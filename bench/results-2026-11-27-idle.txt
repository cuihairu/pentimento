Conclusion: the three missed thresholds do NOT improve under near-idle conditions —
import throughput rises ~26% and still lands at ~40 MiB/s (threshold 100), name
search P95 is actually slightly worse at ~414 ms, and snapshot is unchanged at
~125 s. The three misses are structural (per-file fixed costs, per-asset catalog
queries, unconditional re-hashing of all working copies), not load artifacts. This
strengthens the evidence base for the user's D-20–D-23 decision on P-004.
