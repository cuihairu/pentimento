Interpretation:
- Even with warm-up, no paint, and facts cached on reload, the full-app
  filter-change P95 is 4.1 s and the isolated path is 1.5 s — both far
  above the draft 200 ms budget. The dominant cost is the synchronous
  show/hide of 2,590 QListWidget items in applyFilter; the 2 s change-scan
  timer adds further stalls in the full-app pass (filter apply P95 3.0 s vs
  1.5 s isolated).
- The previous run's numbers (1.6-2.7 s) are retained in
  results-2026-11-27-ui.txt as an earlier estimate; this file is the
  methodologically cleaner measurement.
- D-23's request for user-visible wait and UI responsiveness is satisfied
  with measured numbers, but whether to keep the 200 ms budget, optimize
  applyFilter, or accept a larger budget remains a user decision. P-004
  stays unchecked.
